Historical corrected 1981 edition and its modern companion.

## Files

- `zeta_operators_2026.tex` (Draft 5): the corrected and expanded edition of the 1981 manuscript "Zeta Functions and Their Associated Operators". Its proofs use only tools available in 1981. The 2026 editorial remarks (the November 2026 RH formalization, and Remark 9.4 pointing to the companion spectral-synthesis theorem) are marked as such. Revision log: `revision_log_2026d.tex` (covers Drafts 4 and 5).
- `rh_2026_operator_implications.tex` (Draft 2): what the November 2026 certified formal proof of RH implies, and does not imply, for the summation operators. On S_1 the closed joint kernel is {0}; on S_k with k >= 2 it is spanned by the zero powers of all nontrivial zeros.
- `graph_checks.py`: numerical checks of the graph examples.

See also ../grosswald-schnitzer-graph/.
