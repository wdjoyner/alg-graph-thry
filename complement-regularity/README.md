# Complement–regularity

Project for David Joyner's *Which S_m have a complement in S_n?* (original 1997, revised 2007).

**Draft 4 prepared locally, not yet uploaded to GitHub:** expanded LaTeX/PDF with two TikZ diagrams, graph definitions and proofs, and glossary; latest original source archive; revision log; Python/SymPy and GAP verification scripts.

**Verified independently with SymPy:** the explicit degree-10, degree-11, and degree-12 permutation-generator pairs in the original manuscript have orders 720, 7920, and 95040 and regular orbits on ordered 3-, 4-, and 5-tuples respectively.
