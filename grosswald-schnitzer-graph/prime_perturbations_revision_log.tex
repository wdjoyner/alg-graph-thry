\section{Draft 3 (November 28, 2026), prepared by Claude}
Claude (Anthropic) refereed Draft~2 together with the companion paper
\nolinkurl{grosswald_schnitzer_spectral_invariance_2026.tex}, the
corrected 1981 edition, and the RH companion note.  Claude checked the
proofs of Theorems~4.1 and~5.1, Propositions~6.1, 6.2 and~7.1, and
Corollaries~8.1 and~8.2, and found them correct.  Draft~3 makes the
following changes.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Error corrected.}  Example~3 of Section~2 listed the
  generalized integers for $A=(5/2,3,5,7,\ldots)$ as
  $1,5/2,3,5,25/4,15/2,\ldots$, omitting $7$.  The list now reads
  $1,5/2,3,5,25/4,7,15/2,9,\ldots$.
\item \emph{Companion paper.}  The citation CJ26 (the November~1 version,
  prepared by ChatGPT) is replaced by GS26, the current Draft~2 of the
  spectral-invariance paper in this folder.  Statements that the
  closed-kernel equality ``depends on the closure and domain arguments of
  that working model, which are not independently reverified here'' are
  replaced by citations of its Theorem~2.1, whose proof Claude checked.
  The $H^\infty$ criterion is now cited to Proposition~6.1 there.
\item \emph{RH consequences.}  Section~10 and the Introduction no longer
  say that RH gives no statement about the closed joint kernel.  With
  GS26 Theorem~2.1 and Corollary~11.1, RH gives that the closed joint
  kernel is $\{0\}$ on $S_1$ and is the closed span of the zero powers
  of all nontrivial zeros on $S_k$, $k\ge2$.  The von Koch bounds are no
  longer called ``conditional''.  The RH result is attributed as found
  with Anthropic's Claude, citing Rubinstein's page; the title of that
  citation was corrected and an access date added.
\item \emph{Notation.}  The dilation generator $\Delta=y\,d/dy$, used in
  Section~10, is now defined in the notation table.  Point masses in
  Section~6.3 are written $\varepsilon_x$ instead of $\delta_x$, since
  $\delta$ is the Sobolev threshold.  In Theorem~4.1 the hypothesis
  $0<\delta\le1$ is relaxed to $\delta>0$, with a sentence explaining
  that $\delta>1$ is trivial; this matches Theorem~5.1.  The worked
  example after Theorem~4.1 now states its norm formula for every
  $\delta>0$ and records the numerical value $4.039$ at
  $\delta=\frac12$, which Claude confirmed by direct maximization.
\item \emph{Proposition~7.1.}  The ceiling in $\lceil j^3\rceil$ and the
  clause about initial collisions were removed: cubes are integers and
  are never adjacent.
\item \emph{Cross-reference.}  The graph section refers to
  Corollary~8.1 instead of ``the companion equivalence-class argument''.
  The graph section is now kept only in this paper; the companion paper
  summarizes it and cites it.
\item \emph{Style guide.}  Examples were added to Section~3
  (Example~3.1, the three relations for the endpoint shift) and to
  Section~8 (Example~8.3, computing an inventory with the interior point
  $e$).  The glossary was alphabetized, the entry ``Dilation generator''
  was added, and the joint-kernel entry now uses $r$ instead of $q$.  The
  draft line and date line were simplified, and one overfull line was
  removed.  Theorem numbering is unchanged.
\end{itemize}
