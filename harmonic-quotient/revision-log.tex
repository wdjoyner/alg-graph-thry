\section{Draft 0 to Draft 1 (computational referee check)}

Claude (Anthropic) recomputed every table, morphism count, quotient list and
graph invariant with independent programs (now in \path{verification/}).
The full report is in \path{referee/2026-11-15/}. The line-by-line changes
are in \path{referee/2026-11-15/corrections.diff}. In summary:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Corrected tables that had been copied from neighboring examples:
{\tt d3reg8e} (not harmonic as printed), the {\tt d4reg9a} coantenna table,
the {\tt d4reg12d} butterfly table, and the $m$ column of the {\tt d4reg11f}
$C_6+C_4+C_4$ table.
\item Corrected stated subgroups that did not give their tables:
{\tt d3reg10k}, {\tt d3reg10r}, {\tt d3reg10s}, and $G_1$, $G_3$ in
{\tt d3reg12h}.
\item Corrected $16$ of $491$ stated morphism counts, including all counts
for {\tt d3reg6a} and {\tt d3reg6b} and the counts for {\tt d4reg11f},
{\tt d4reg11g}, {\tt d3reg12a}, {\tt d3reg12g} and {\tt d5reg8a}.
\item Added the quotient for $G=\mathrm{Aut}(\Gamma_2)$ in $19$ examples. The
Sage function \path{has_harmonic_quotient} omits it.
\item Corrected $\deg(\phi)$ in nine tables, where $|G|$ or the fiber size
had been recorded instead of $\sum m_\phi(x)$ over a fiber.
\item Corrected wrong $\nu$, $m$ and genus entries, quotient breakdowns,
graph identifications, five characteristic polynomial coefficients, two
girths, one critical group order, LCF entries in the summary tables, two
census totals, and the statement of Tait's conjecture.
\end{enumerate}
No result changed status. All corrected statements are computational
results, as before.

\subsection{Corrections made in Draft 2}
\label{sec:d2-corrections}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Example 6.1 ({\tt d3reg8a}) said that $K_4$ is a matchstick graph.
This is false: a matchstick graph is a unit-distance graph, and the plane
contains no four points at mutual distance $1$. The sentence now says this.
\item Removed a stray formula fragment after the subgroup in Example 6.18
({\tt d3reg10m}), and displayed the subgroup.
\item Typos: ``There are are'', ``it's automorphism group''.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Copyedits by David Joyner incorporated in Draft 2}

Commit \texttt{12b0ae8} (November 16, 2026) copyedited Draft 1. Its diff is
\path{regular-graph-revision-2026-11-16.diff}. All of its changes are in
Draft 2, apart from those that Draft 2 had already made in another way (the

products, and $34680=2^3\cdot 3\cdot 5\cdot 17^2$ now shows its factor $3$.

morphism tables.

\item The page reference ``See also page \ldots\ below'' pointed to a label
above it. Draft 2 now refers to Example~7.18 ({\tt d4reg9h}).
\end{itemize}
